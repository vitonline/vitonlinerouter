# VitonlineRouter brand

Product name: VitonlineRouter. Tagline: Reliable AI routing for your coding tools.

Positioning: a self-hosted gateway for customer-owned AI accounts, with a small tested model pool, clear diagnostics, bounded fallback and optional setup/support.

Use brand.json as the product-name and tagline source of truth; run the brand sync command after changes. Keep the upstream MIT copyright and license notices. Avoid “unlimited free AI,” “all models verified,” exact savings percentages, or model-lineage claims without evidence. Provider-published IDs and custom aliases describe routing; they do not verify the model weights behind an endpoint.

Primary onboarding model: RecommendedModels, once at least three models qualify. Advanced/experimental model: MyDefaultProvider. The paid pilot should explain the customer's own provider billing and supported integrations.

Tone: direct, calm and specific. Show why a request failed and what the customer can do next. Say “estimated cost” and “unknown pricing” explicitly. Make connection credentials, conversation retention and diagnostic sharing visible choices.
